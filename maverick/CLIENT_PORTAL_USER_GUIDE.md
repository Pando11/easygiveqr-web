# Maverick Client Portal User Guide

This guide is for buyers and sellers who receive a secure Maverick client portal link.

## What the portal includes

- Transaction progress bar
- Timeline of key milestones
- Document checklist (received vs pending)
- Upload area for signed documents

## Accessing the portal

1. Open the secure link sent by text/email from Maverick.
2. The link looks like:
   - `https://<your-domain>/client/<access_token>`
3. Save the link for future updates.

## Timeline tab

- Shows upcoming and completed milestones.
- Overdue milestones are highlighted.
- Use this tab to track where your file stands.

## Documents tab

### View documents
- Open **Documents** to see available files.
- Click **View** to open a document securely.

### Upload signed documents

1. Go to **Documents**.
2. Choose a document type.
3. Select your file (PDF/JPG/JPEG/PNG).
4. Click **Upload Document**.
5. Confirm the success banner appears.

## Supported upload types

- Signed Amendment
- Signed Disclosure
- Signed Addendum
- Insurance Binder
- Loan Approval
- Settlement Statement
- Other

## Security notes

- Your portal URL is unique and should not be shared publicly.
- If you think the link was shared accidentally, contact Maverick TC to regenerate it.

## Need help?

If you cannot access the portal or upload documents, contact your Maverick coordinator and request a refreshed portal link.
