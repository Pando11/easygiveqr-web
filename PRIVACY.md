# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** January 2026

## Overview

EasyGiveQR ("we", "our", "us") is a donation platform that enables churches to accept donations through QR codes and online forms. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and how we protect it.

## Data We Collect

### Church Information

We store the following information about churches using our platform:
- Church identification (church ID, legal name, display name)
- Contact information (admin email addresses)
- Tax identification (EIN)
- Church preferences (language, colors, branding)
- Stripe Connect account information (for payment processing)

### Donation Information

For each donation, we store:
- Donation amount and currency
- Payment status
- Donation date and time
- Church ID (to associate donation with church)
- Stripe session ID (for reconciliation)

### Donor Information (Optional)

If provided during checkout:
- Donor email address
- Donor name
- Donor mailing address (for tax receipt purposes)

**Note:** We do NOT store:
- Credit card numbers
- Full payment card details
- CVV codes
- Bank account information

All payment processing is handled by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. We never see or store full payment card data.

### Engagement Submissions

For prayer requests, visitor connect cards, and volunteer interest forms:
- Name (optional for prayer requests)
- Email address (optional)
- Phone number (optional)
- Message content
- Submission date and time

## How We Use Your Data

### Donation Processing

- Process donations through Stripe
- Generate tax receipts (annual)
- Send weekly donation summaries to church administrators
- Reconcile payments and track donation history

### Church Services

- Generate QR codes for donation links
- Send email notifications to church administrators
- Provide church-specific branding and customization

### Engagement Forms

- Forward prayer requests, visitor information, and volunteer interest to church administrators
- Send email notifications to church administrators

## Data Retention

### Donation Records

We retain donation records for **7 years** to comply with tax and accounting requirements. This retention period may be adjusted based on legal requirements.

### Church Information

Church information is retained as long as the church account is active. Upon account closure, data may be retained for up to 7 years for legal and accounting purposes.

### Engagement Submissions

Engagement submissions are retained until marked as "completed" by church administrators. Completed submissions may be retained for up to 2 years.

## Data Security

- All data is stored in secure, encrypted databases (Supabase)
- Payment processing is handled by Stripe (PCI-compliant)
- Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel only
- We use industry-standard security practices to protect your information
